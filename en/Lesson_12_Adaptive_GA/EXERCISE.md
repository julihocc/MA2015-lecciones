# Lesson 12 exercise: crossover and mutation grid

Reuse `src/tsp_06_a_tuned_rival.py` with a wider grid of crossover and mutation
settings. Keep `RUNS = 12` and `BUDGET = 12_000`; change no other teaching code.

1. Copy the script so the lesson source remains unchanged.
2. Evaluate all combinations of crossover in `(0.20, 0.40, 0.60, 0.90)` and
   mutation in `(0.05, 0.15, 0.25, 0.30)`.
3. Predict the runtime before running the grid.
4. Compare the mean route length of each fixed setting with the adaptive regime,
   report the best setting, and explain the uncertainty in the comparison.

Treat the result as a descriptive experiment, not as a significance test.
