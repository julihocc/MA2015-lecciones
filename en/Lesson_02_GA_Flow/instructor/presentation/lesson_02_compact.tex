\lessontitle{02}{Genetic algorithm flow}

\begin{frame}{Problem and target}

Maximize $f(x)=-(x-2)^2$ on $[-4,4]$. The known maximum is $f(2)=0$. A population stores candidates; each evaluation costs one call to $f$. This example is synthetic.

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{One generation and stopping}

$P_t\;\longrightarrow\;$ selection $\longrightarrow$ crossover $\longrightarrow$ mutation $\longrightarrow$ replacement $\longrightarrow P_{t+1}$. The controller evaluates and decides when to stop. One generation is not an entire run.

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Selection and creation}

A two-way tournament copies the fitter candidate from a random pair. Parents $1$ and $3$ average to $2$. Mutation adds a random displacement; clip the result to $[-4,4]$. Only crossover and mutation propose new genes.

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Problem as an argument}

The controller accepts a function $f$; the landscape is not hard-coded in the loop. Switching from $-(x-2)^2$ to another evaluator changes fitness, not the five phases. Minimizing $c(x)$ can mean maximizing $-c(x)$ while preserving constraints.

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Measure population and cost}

For each generation record current best $\max f(P_t)$, mean $\overline f_t$, and spread $\sigma(P_t)$. Keep the all-time best separately. Count evaluations, not only generations, as the budget.

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Stopping has limited meaning}

A patience rule stops after several generations without meaningful improvement in the all-time best. It detects observed stagnation, not convergence or a global optimum. Without elitism, the last population may lose its archived champion.

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{The stepped case}

In the extensive example, the top step is $0.5$ wide and the run reaches the grid reference. A flat step alone does not prove that patience fails. Comparing against a reference and budget prevents a false conclusion.

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Transfer to Planta Física}

A plan has 48 decisions; its evaluator returns scalar fitness and checks feasibility. Compare the GA with random search using the same evaluator and budget. Question: what do you archive when replacing the entire population?

\end{frame}
